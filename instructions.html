Germy Warfare — How to Play

Meet Bill. He's a virus with big dreams: total world domination, one fibroblast at a time.

Bill's plan is simple: infect every last fibroblast in the body, multiply like crazy, and become unstoppable. The immune system? Just a bunch of overachievers trying to ruin his vibe.

We believe in Bill. You believe in Bill. Let's make it happen. 💜

The Goal

Infect fibroblasts. Survive the immune system. Take over the world.

Each level lasts 60 seconds. Survive to the end and Bill advances his conquest!

Controls

  • Mouse — Move Bill (you are the cursor!)
  • Space — Shoot a glycoprotein (infects cells or kills enemies)
  • Shift / Click — LYSIS: burst all infected fibroblasts at once (score + ammo!)

The World

Fibroblasts are Bill's favorite snack. Float around, minding their own business. Shoot them to infect — they turn bright green and become Bill's loyal minions. When you have a bunch infected, lyse them to pop them all for massive points and ammo!

DNA drops when infected fibroblasts die. Collect 10 DNA and Bill earns an extra life. Because Bill deserves it.

The Immune System (Bill's Annoying Fans)

These guys think they're the good guys. Let's prove them wrong.

Macrophages — Big red brutes that chase Bill. Touch = lose a life. Shooting one makes it burst into 4 more (because of course it does). They scream "OW!" when it happens.
B Cells — Patient snipers. They hang back and fire sticky antibodies at Bill. 5 stuck to him = game over (Bill gets very uncomfortable). Touching one directly also gets Bill stuck.
Eosinophils — The "good cop" of the immune system. They try to cure infected fibroblasts (rude!). Touch = lose a life. At least they're slow.
Neutrophils — Lazy workers. They drift around converting fibroblasts into more neutrophils. Touch = lose a life. Boring, but dangerous if ignored.
Helper T Cells — Smug teacher's pets. They chase Bill with a self-satisfied attitude. Touch = lose a life. They think they're better than you.
Cytotoxic T Cells — Fast, zig-zagging assassins. Touch = lose a life. Very rude.
Basophils — Big and slow but they release histamine clouds. Touch = lose a life. Smells bad.
Dendritic Cells — Patient trappers. They drift slowly and place cytokine mines near Bill. Touch one = lose a life. They can't be killed — just get angry when shot.
Natural Killer Ninjas — Stealthy assassins. Chase Bill and blink away from shots. Teleport dramatically every 10 seconds. Touch = lose a life.
Myeloid Progenitor — The queen cell. Drifts slowly toward Bill while her monocytes orbit in protective ellipses. Touch either = lose a life. When she dies, they scatter and attack.
Hematopoietic Stem Cell — Ancient boss. Spawns waves of immune cells in escalating phases. Touch = lose a life. The source of it all.

Scoring & Lives

  • Lysing infected cells = points (more cells = more points)
  • Lysing many at once = big ammo bonus (exponential!)
  • 3 lives to start (shown as Bill faces). Lose one on touch by most immune cells.
  • 10 DNA = +1 life. Bill's reward for a job well done.
  • Accuracy & grade shown on level-complete screen.

Pro Tips (for Bill's Success)

  • Don't shoot macrophages unless you're ready for 4 more.
  • B Cells respawn after you kill them. They don't forget.
  • LYSIS is your best friend. Infect first, burst later.
  • Collect DNA. Always collect DNA.
