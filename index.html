The Story Begins

Bill is a virus. A very ambitious one.

He dreams of taking over the entire body — one fibroblast at a time. Fibroblasts are the soft, defenseless cells that hold everything together. Bill infects them. They become his. And when he has enough? He multiplies. Spreads. Conquers.

But the immune system doesn't just sit there and watch. They fight back. Bill's journey starts now. The fibroblasts await. The immune system is watching.

How to Play

Mouse — Move Bill (you ARE the cursor)
Space — Shoot a glycoprotein (infects fibroblasts / damages enemies)
Shift or Click — LYSIS: burst all infected fibroblasts at once (score + ammo!)
