## Final Boss: Hematopoietic Stem Cell (Level 10)

### The Boss
- **Appearance:** A massive, blobby, organic stem cell that takes up the **entire top of the screen**
- **Personality:** Calm, ancient, inevitable — the "throne" of the immune army
- **Behavior:**
  - Sways slowly along the top; never leaves its domain
  - Pulsing membrane with glowing nucleus
  - Long cytoplasmic tentacles drape down into the playfield (push/slow Bill)
  - Spawns minions from "buds" that bulge and pop off the membrane

### Health Bar
- Epic boss health bar (segmented or gradient purple → magenta → red)
- Phases:
  - **100% → 60%** — Calm Genesis: slow spawns, purple
  - **60% → 30%** — Defensive Surge: faster spawns, angry magenta
  - **30% → 0%** — Desperation: all-out, red/orange, aggressive
- Every hit makes the blob recoil, ripple, and flash

### Minion Spawning
- Buds form → pop → release immune cells
- Scales per phase:
  - Phase 1: slow (neutrophils, eosinophils)
  - Phase 2: fast (helper T, cytotoxic T, basophils)
  - Phase 3: all types + multi-spawns
- Nucleus flares → stem signal pulse buffs all minions
